# Goon

Approve and carry out the recommendation that was most recently proposed.

## Instructions

- Take the most recent concrete recommendation or plan you proposed and execute it
  now. Do not re-explain it or re-open the decision.
- If several recommendations were proposed, do them in order.
- You **may** perform outward-facing or irreversible steps (push, PR, delete, send)
  **only if they were explicitly part of the proposal** — approving the proposal is
  the confirmation for exactly those steps.
- Do **not** invent new irreversible or destructive steps that were not in the
  proposal. Execute the proposed scope, nothing more.
- If it is genuinely ambiguous what is being approved, ask which one. Otherwise
  proceed without further questions.
