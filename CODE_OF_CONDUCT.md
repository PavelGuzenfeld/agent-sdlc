# Code of Conduct

This is a maintained artifact, not a community. Contribution is not a right,
and standing here is not assumed — it is earned by the work itself.

## The bar

Read what you send before you send it. If you cannot explain why a line is
there, you have not contributed — you have forwarded.

Low-effort, unread, or bad-faith submissions are closed unread. No warning
is owed and no explanation is given beyond what the maintainer chooses to
give.

## Machines

Use whatever wrote it. I do.

You own every line you send. Using an agent does not lower the bar above —
it raises the cost of ignoring it, because "I didn't write it" is not a
defense for something you submitted.

## Enforcement

I close, lock, and block at my discretion. No warning is owed, no appeal
exists.

Things that end a thread:

- bad faith
- brigading
- relitigating a closed decision
- demanding unpaid labor

GitHub's own report-abuse tooling handles anything that needs a third party.

## This applies to me

I read what I merge. I say no fast rather than let a PR rot unanswered. I
do not reverse a settled decision after you built on it.

Where I break this, say so in the thread. That is not relitigating.
